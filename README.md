## 🦠 Epiphase — an Ecosystem for Infectious Disease Modeling & Intervention Assessment

My core work: an integrated toolkit that links mechanistic epidemic models with change-point detection, AI, and LLM-assisted modeling.

| Component | What does |
|---|---|
| **Epiphase+** | High-dimensional framework for comparing epidemic phases and intervention scenarios |
| **MICA** | Model-Informed Change-point Analysis — detects *parameter-level* shifts in dynamical models (binary segmentation + genetic algorithm), validated on COVID-19 intervention data & industrial monitoring · [bioRxiv 2026](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.03.16.712011) |
| **breakpointprofile** | Profiling structural break points in epidemic time series for intervention assessment |
| **LLM integration** | Connecting the ecosystem to LLMs — for model building (equation/parameter extraction from literature) and for interpretation (natural-language analysis of model behavior & intervention impact) |

## 🔬 Research Lines

**Intracellular Virus Life Cycle & Model Validation**
Mechanistic modeling of viral transcription–replication phase transitions (Ebola); calibration and validation against perturbation minigenome datasets.

**Machine Learning / AI**
- **VectorSage** — semantic + lexical retrieval for large-scale PubMed search · [Bioinformatics Advances 2026](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioadv/vbag116)
- **Cascade multimodal models** — energy-aware early-infection warning: continuous vital-sign risk model activates audio/disease classifiers only after an alert

**Biostatistics**
Clinical trial design & analysis · survival analysis · statistical methods for biomedical & biometry research.
